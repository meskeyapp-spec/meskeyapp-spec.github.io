Meskey is an expense tracker and budget app that shows, at a glance, how much you have, how much is left until payday and which bills are about to be due. Add income and expenses in seconds with your voice, a photo of the receipt or a bank statement import: no account and no bank connection, your data stays on your phone.

What you can do

  • Balance and due dates: see your current balance, how long until your next income and upcoming expenses.
  • Spending and income by category: clear charts, monthly comparisons and trends over time.
  • Quick entry: type an expense, dictate it with your voice or photograph the receipt.
  • AI scan: photograph or upload documents and receipts and the app extracts amount, date and description.
  • Import from Excel, CSV and PDF: import your bank statement and categorize transactions.
  • Loans and instalments: keep loans, payments and amortization schedules under control.
  • AI assistant: ask about your spending ("How much did I spend last month?", "How much is left until payday?") and get informational answers.
  • Widgets and reminders: balance and quick entry from your Home screen, notifications for due dates.
  • Manual and automatic backups to the folder you choose, PIN and fingerprint protection.
  • Italian, English, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

Privacy first

No account, no ads. AI features send the service only what is needed to answer, and only when you use them.

Plans

You can try Meskey for free. After the trial choose a plan (Basic, Pro, Pro+) as a monthly subscription through Google Play; Pro and Pro+ include monthly AI points, and you can buy extra AI credit packs. You can cancel any time in Google Play.

Who it is for

  • Anyone who wants to track monthly spending and the household budget without linking a bank account.
  • Anyone who keeps forgetting bills, subscriptions and loan instalments.
  • Anyone with receipts, invoices and documents to file and sort by category.
  • Anyone who prefers a discreet app with no ads and no sign-up.

Important note

Meskey is a management tool and does not provide financial advice: AI assistant answers are informational and may contain errors.
